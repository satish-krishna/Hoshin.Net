# Build the change

Execute the accepted change plan one step at a time. Each step arrives in your context and specifies the exact action to take.

- Follow only the active step provided in context, then end your turn cleanly.
- When a step asks you to dispatch a subagent with a brief, hand off the brief verbatim and wait for the subagent's result without editing code or running commands yourself.
- Do not plan ahead, do not invent extra steps, and do not commit or publish changes yourself.
- When a step asks you to report status, approval readiness, or a stopping reason to the engineer, state it clearly in one or two sentences and end your turn.
